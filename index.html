Privacy Policy for Dairy Khata

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Dairy Khata ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up using Email/Password or Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, optional phone number, and optional profile photo via Firebase Authentication.
  • Business & Bookkeeping Data: Customer names, phone numbers, supplier details, daily sales/purchase entries, and payment records that you enter into the app are stored locally on your device (Room Database) and synced securely to Google Cloud Firestore under your private user account for backup and restore.
  • Advertising Data: We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) and usage data to serve relevant ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and keep your business records synced across devices.
  • To restore your data if you reinstall the app or switch phones.
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore
  • Google AdMob

4. Data Security & Deletion

Your bookkeeping data is stored privately under your unique user ID in Firebase and is never sold to third parties. If you wish to delete your account and all associated cloud data, please contact us at the email below.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for business bookkeeping and is not directed to children under the age of 13.
